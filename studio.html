The Studio

Independent horror games by Ayman Jbari and AJB Games Studio.

Cursed castles, haunted dolls, and family homes where something is deeply wrong.

AJB GAMES STUDIO was founded by Ayman Jbari, based in Morocco. The studio makes horror games — from first-person castle crawls to haunted mascot toys — and is now working on its next project.

Winner of the Coup de Coeur award at the LGaming Awards 2023.
