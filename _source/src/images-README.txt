Put your own pictures here and they are used automatically (they win over the Steam images).

logo.png                  the AJB logo (transparent PNG, about 400px wide)
<game>-header.jpg         card picture, 460x215 or larger (Steam "header capsule")
<game>-hero.jpg           wide picture for the top of the game page, 1920x620 or larger

<game> is one of:
  martha-s-dolls-2
  uncursed
  bokkie
  martha-s-dolls
  the-moroccan-castle-3
  cazafonia

Example: uncursed-header.jpg, uncursed-hero.jpg
Missing files are skipped. The page then uses the Steam picture, and if that fails, a generated dark scene.
